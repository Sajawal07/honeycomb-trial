Honeycomb Trial

An endless needle-tracing survival game

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Honeycomb Trial ("the Game"), developed by Sajawal07. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you play our mobile game available on the Google Play Store.

Short version: Honeycomb Trial does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All game progress is saved locally on your device only.

2. Information We Collect

Honeycomb Trial is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data transmitted to our servers (the game has no server)

All game data (score, coins, rounds played, discovered designs, settings) is stored exclusively in your device's local storage and never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party frameworks and platform services:

  • Google Play Services — required for app distribution via the Google Play Store. Google may collect device information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apache Capacitor — an open-source framework that bridges web code with native Android APIs. It does not independently collect any data.

We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social login services.

4. Permissions

The game requests the following Android permission:

  • INTERNET — required by the Capacitor framework for loading the web-based game content locally within the app. The game does not make any outbound network requests during gameplay.
  • VIBRATE — used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay (can be disabled in Settings). No data is collected.

5. Children's Privacy

Honeycomb Trial does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Since the game collects no personal data whatsoever, it is safe for players of all ages.

6. Data Security

Because all game data is stored locally on your device, security of that data is governed by your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We have no access to your game data and therefore cannot breach it.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all game data at any time in two ways:

  • Inside the game: Go to Settings → Reset Progress to clear all saved scores, coins, and discoveries.
  • On your device: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Honeycomb Trial → Clear Data to remove all local storage.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored data permanently.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
